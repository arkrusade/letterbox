POSTCARD ANSWER STUDY

Seven ways to send
a little piece of your day.

Tap or focus a postcard and press Enter to turn it over. Compare photo-led fronts, roomy handwriting, travel details, and a longer three-part note.

01

A little window

A travel snapshot beside the note

Best when a photo or place is part of the story.

02

Just the note

Room to linger over the handwriting

Best when the words are the keepsake.

03

A note & address

Borrow the layout of a mailed postcard

Best for a nostalgic, mail-ready feel.

04

A day in the journal

Place, weather, and a longer letter

A longer answer, with small details to remember the day.

05

Three little views

A tiny photo tableau with a note

A visual answer for a walk, trip, or ordinary day.

06

All in handwriting

One uninterrupted page of letter-writing

Like finding a real letter tucked in the mailbox.

07

A keepsake stamp

Turn a favorite moment into a stamp

A scenic image framed like a collectible postage stamp.

✳

Another direction to try: make the answer side feel like a little travel journal—with a date, a tiny “weather today” note, and one handwritten line that stood out—while keeping the full response readable on the front.
